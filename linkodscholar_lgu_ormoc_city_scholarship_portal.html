Qualifications & Disqualifications

Official eligibility criteria and disqualification grounds set by the LGU Ormoc City Scholarship Office.

Qualifications (8 Criteria)

1

Must be a Filipino citizen.

2

Applicant and his/her family must be permanent residents of the City of Ormoc.

3

With good moral character and without any derogatory record.

4

For Grade 12 / Senior High School Graduates: Must have at least an 80% general average and no failing grade in any subject during Grade 11 (and must graduate from Senior High School upon admission), or be a Senior High School graduate with at least an 80% average and no failing grade in Grade 12.

5

For Currently Enrolled College Students: Must have a weighted average of at least 2.5 or 80% in the semester prior to application, with no failing grade and no incomplete (INC) grade.

6

Has passed the entrance examination, if required, of the school where he/she intends to enroll.

7

Comes from a low-income / underprivileged family in Ormoc City, particularly those classified among the poorest of the poor.

8

Not presently enjoying any other government or private scholarship or study grant.

Disqualifications (5 Grounds)

1

Persons who have already attained a bachelor’s degree or equivalent degree from any public or private higher education institution.

2

Persons who have been dishonorably discharged from any public or private higher education institution for any reason other than financial difficulty in paying tuition and fees.

3

Persons who have been convicted of any crime involving moral turpitude.

4

Persons coming from a family with a member who is already a grantee of the city scholarship program and has finished his/her academic program.

5

Persons who were previously disqualified or terminated from any scholarship grant.

Official Requirements

Review the documents and actions needed throughout the LGU Ormoc City Scholarship application.

Application + Initial Requirements

Online Submission
  • Completed online application form (personal, family, and educational information)
  • 2x2 ID picture (uploaded)
  • Barangay Clearance (uploaded)
  • Certificate of Indigency (uploaded)
  • Applicant’s signature and parent or guardian’s signature (uploaded)

Qualifying Examination

Offline / In Person
  • Attend the physical Qualifying Examination on the scheduled date and venue assigned by the LGU Ormoc City Scholarship Office
  • Official examination results are encoded into the system by Scholarship Office administrators

CSWD Interview

Offline / In Person
  • Attend the scheduled interview conducted through the City Social Welfare and Development (CSWD) office
  • Official interview results are encoded into the system by Scholarship Office administrators

City Hall Interview

Offline / In Person
  • Attend the physical City Hall Interview on the scheduled date and batch
  • Passing this interview unlocks final document submission

Final Document Submission

Physical Submission + Portal Upload

Personally submit the physical documents to the LGU Ormoc City Scholarship Office and upload a clear photo/scan of each on your applicant dashboard:

  • Printed and signed application form (printed from your applicant dashboard)
  • Certificate of Good Moral Character from the school (previous year, certified true copy)
  • Report Card (Form 138, previous year) for Grade 12 and 1st Year College; OR Certificate of Grades from the previous year for 2nd to 3rd Year College (certified true copy by the school)
  • Official Report of Ratings or Certificate of Grades for the last semester attended, issued by the School Registrar or Dean (original; general average must not be lower than 2.5 or 80% for those with college units)
  • Latest BIR Income Tax Return (ITR) or Certificate of Tax Exemption of parents/guardian; OR an Affidavit of Low Income if not filing an ITR (original)
  • Certification of the basic salary of the applicant’s parent(s) from HR, if employed (original)
  • Case Assessment Certificate from CSWD (Barangay 3-in-1 Building)
  • Electric bill and water bill for the past month (photocopy)

60-Hour Community Service Obligation

How active scholars fulfill their annual civic service requirement through the LinkodScholar mobile app.

Annual Requirement
60 Hours / Year

Divided into 30 hours per semester across LGU-organized volunteer activities.

Credited Activities
Community Service Only

Clean-ups, tree planting, bloodletting drives, and donation/relief operations count toward the 60-hour quota.

Attendance-Only Events
Meetings & Seminars

Meetings, seminars, trainings, orientations, and workshops record attendance but do not credit service hours.

Venue Attendance Verification (Online & Offline Support)

Scholars check in and check out at activity venues using the dedicated LinkodScholar mobile app, which verifies presence via GPS coordinates, geofencing boundaries, auto-timestamps, and a geotagged selfie (no facial recognition). When an activity site has weak or no mobile internet signal, attendance logs save locally on the device via SQLite and sync automatically once connectivity returns.
